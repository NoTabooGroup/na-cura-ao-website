New to NA?

Attending your first meeting can feel like a big step.

You don't need an appointment, and you don't have to speak.

Many people start by simply listening and getting to know others who understand.

The only requirement for membership is a desire to stop using.

What to Expect at a Meeting

NA meetings are a place where people can share their experience, strength, and hope with each other.

At a meeting you can expect:

  • A safe and respectful environment
  • People who understand your experience
  • No appointments needed
  • No cost to attend

About Narcotics Anonymous

Narcotics Anonymous is a worldwide fellowship of people who help each other stay clean and recover from the disease of addiction.

NA meetings are available in many countries around the world and are open to anyone with a desire to stop using.
